How ranking works

  • Relevance: how close a job is to what you typed, plus a boost for your department.
  • Earn: more weekly hours and Student Helper jobs score higher as “earn” goes up.
  • Learn: the AI-rated job level and internship schemes score higher as “learn” goes up.

Closed jobs are hidden. Every result shows why it ranked where it did.
