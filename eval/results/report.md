## What was tuned on dev, and what was chosen

Tuned with `eval/tune.py` on the 40 dev queries only, on nDCG@10. To avoid chasing noise, the simpler or existing setting was kept unless an alternative was better by at least 0.005. Every result the tuning looked at (top 10 of every variant) was judged first, so no variant was penalised for unjudged results.

The numbers in this table are the ones the decisions were made on. They differ a little from the dev table above: the final pool (top 20 of every system) later added judged listings for the dev queries too, which changes each query's ideal ranking, and the committed bge-m3 vectors are stored as float16. `compact_N` = one compact text with N characters of description; `chunks_N` = pieces of N characters, best piece counts.
